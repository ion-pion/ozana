Ozana — a web browser written in Oberon, on a phone

Ozana reads the web herself: the pages, their words, links, forms and pictures are taken apart in Oberon‑07 and drawn by the phone's graphics card. She was written and compiled on a Samsung Galaxy A15, without a computer, with Byz7 (OLL2, an Oberon‑07 → LLVM compiler that runs on the phone).

Her name: in Romanian “Ozana” sounds like “o zână”, a fairy; it is also the river of Ion Creangă's childhood. Here she is a fairy who lives on her own. She is the same fairy as Nora, the browser inside Pixipogo (Cubicum N), the small operating system in Oberon from the same workshop.

What she does

Pages, read in Oberon

Her own HTML reader and layout: headings, paragraphs, lists, links, tables as text, forms (GET and POST), bookmarks, history, find in the page, copy and paste, instant BACK from pages kept on the phone.

Pictures, decoded in Oberon

JPEG (baseline and progressive), PNG (with Adam7), GIF and WEBP, read by her own decoders (Pic6). Colours from the 4 nearest samples, the WEBP loop filter, transparency kept or laid on white.

On the graphics card

Each picture becomes a texture (OllGL7). Then it costs two triangles a frame and is drawn sharp, at the real resolution of the screen.

Films and sounds

MP4, WebM, MP3 and the like play in her own player. Films given freely (PeerTube, the Internet Archive, Wikimedia Commons) are found and read in Oberon (Filme1) and play in it too.

The whole web, when needed

YouTube, TikTok, Vimeo and Twitch are JavaScript. They open in a box where the phone's own web engine runs the site and its own player. A YouTube film goes to NewPipe with one button.

Like the big ones

Up to 8 tabs, private tabs (no history), saved pages to read without the net, Share, downloads of any size by the phone's downloader, the sites as on a computer.

On the phone

How she is made

Nora18The browser: the HTML reader, the layout, links, forms, tabs, history, marks, saved pages, the player's buttons, the box into the whole web.
Pic6The picture decoders: JPEG, PNG, GIF, WEBP (VP8 with its loop filter), down to the pixels the screen needs.
Filme1A small JSON reader and the free film sources: PeerTube (Sepia Search), the Internet Archive, Wikimedia Commons.
OllGL73D and 2D drawing on the graphics card, now with textures: Poza2D, Bucata2D, Sprite2D (turned, tinted, transparent).
Motor2 · Desk6 · Keys1 · Gazda4The frame loop, the look and the colours, the keyboard, the files between the app and the phone.
the window (dex)The thin Android part, spoken to through small files: the network, the graphics card (GLES 2), the film player, the box into the whole web, downloads, Share.

Build it

In Byz7, with the window ecran-cubn49.dex copied as ecran.dex in its tools folder:

OLL2.Apk Motor2.Mod OllGL4.Mod OllGL7.Mod OllGL6t.Mod Synth9.Mod Text1.Mod Keys1.Mod Fs2.Mod Gazda4.Mod Desk6.Mod Pic6.Mod Filme1.Mod Nora18.Mod Ozana5.Mod "Ozana 5"

The result is an Android app with its own name and icon. Most of it is native ARM64 code compiled from Oberon; the window is the Android part.

What she does not do

  • Her own engine runs no JavaScript and keeps no cookies: sites that are only JavaScript, and logging in, go through the box into the whole web (the phone's own engine).
  • YouTube's films are not taken from YouTube by her: they play in YouTube's own player, in the box, or in NewPipe if it is on the phone.
  • Pages are shown as text, links and pictures, without the sites' own CSS layout.
  • WEBP without loss and animated pictures are not read yet.
